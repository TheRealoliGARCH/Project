# Theory Strengthening Map

The strengthened theory introduces an observation-theoretic layer without yet assigning it a new U-number.

$$
\mathcal E_0=\mathcal A\cap\mathcal D\cap\mathcal W\cap\mathcal I
$$

becomes, under observed information $S$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E^\star(S)=\mathcal E_0\cap\mathcal O(S)}.
$$

The conceptual chain is:

$$
\text{algebraic admissibility}
\to
\text{structural equilibrium}
\to
\text{uniqueness}
\to
\text{identification}
\to
\text{observational recoverability}.
$$

The new concepts are deliberately separated:

| Concept | Mathematical object | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Existence | $\mathcal E^\star(S)\neq\varnothing$ | Is any compatible state possible? |
| Structural uniqueness | $|\mathcal E_0|=1$ | Is the structural equilibrium unique? |
| Observational equivalence | $[x]_{\mathcal H}$ | Which states generate the same observations? |
| Statistical identification | posterior/likelihood map | Can competing states be distinguished probabilistically? |
| Observational recoverability | $\operatorname{diam}(\mathcal C_\delta)\to0$ | Do observations collapse the compatible set? |

The uploaded Theory of Financial Triangulation supplies the motivating framework: heterogeneous observations, source localization on a partially observed network, entropy reduction, Bayesian updating, and information fusion. The Project generalizes those ideas from identifying a hidden financial source to recovering a latent sovereign equilibrium.

## Proposed theorem program

1. **Epistemic Monotonicity:** additional correctly specified information weakly reduces conditional entropy.
2. **Local Observational Identification:** full-rank observation Jacobian on the admissible tangent space is sufficient for local identification under regularity conditions.
3. **Global Observational Recoverability:** injectivity of the observation operator on the structural equilibrium set is sufficient for global deterministic recovery.
4. **Networked Recoverability:** characterize how country-specific, bilateral, and common signals jointly identify the coupled Greece--India--Italy state.

These should become executable mathematical claims only after their assumptions, domains, and numerical tolerances are explicitly encoded in tests.
